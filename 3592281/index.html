Editor preview can start at the wrong position after page height changes

Switching from a long page to a short page in the Pages panel could leave the top of the short page above the editor pane. The fix opts the editor pane out of browser scroll anchoring with overflow-anchor: none. These clips show the same scripted switch on the same pages, before and after the fix.

Pages panel: long page → short page → long page

What these clips show, and what they don't

  • Pages: local test pages Review: long page (1608px preview) and Review: short page (768px). The short page was opened once off camera before recording, so its data was cached. That's when the bug reliably appears.
  • Conditions: Chrome for Testing 153, headless, 1920 × 996, Canvas zoom 100%, Tablet viewport. Recorded 2026-11-03 with the same script for both builds. The baseline misaligned in every rehearsal and take (4/4); the fix aligned in every one (4/4).
  • Why 370px: the preview ends up 50px minus half the height difference from the pane's top: 50 − (1608 − 768) / 2 = −370. The decision guide's earlier measurements (2026-11-02) show 289–297px because the long page was 1462px then. It grew after the demo content push replaced the site's global CSS.
  • Not shown: the rarer case where a short page loads 8px too low. It comes from the same mechanism and was measured separately, but isn't in these clips.
  • Not reproducible on demo content: switching between the starter's Home and Login pages didn't misalign on the baseline (0/5), so those pages aren't used as evidence.
  • Browsers: Chromium only. Firefox and Safari weren't tested.

Measured results

Scenario Before After
Pages panel, long → short (2026-11-02) Misaligned 8/8 0/8
Short page reloads at 1.5× CPU slowdown 8px low in about 3% of loads 0/40
Editing above the visible area, viewport, zoom and window changes while scrolled (2026-11-03) Identical scroll and landmark positions in both builds

The cause, the tested tradeoff and the remaining unknowns are in the decision guide.

Check it yourself

  1. Create a long page and a short page in Canvas.
  2. Open the long page, then switch to the short page and back once in the Pages panel.
  3. From the long page, switch to the short page again. Its top should sit just below the top of the editor pane, not above it.
  4. Switch back to the long page, then scroll the editor, change the viewport and zoom, and confirm the position you scrolled to stays put.
